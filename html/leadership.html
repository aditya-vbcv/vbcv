Messages

Words from the leadership of the Vidyalaya Managing Committee.

Chairman

Mr. Sanjay Sinha

Hon'y Chairman, Vidyalaya Managing Committee

Hari Om

My humble salutations to Param Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayanandaji, Pujya Guruji Swami Tejomayanandaji and Pujya Swami Swaroopanandaji, Head – Chinmaya Mission Worldwide.

Congratulations to Ms Vipin Sharma, the faculty members and the students on the successful completion of 40 years — the Ruby Jubilee.

My association with Vidya Bharati Chinmaya Vidyalaya has reinforced my view that the school aims at creating global citizens who will remain optimistic, ethical and socially responsible. The school fraternity's firm conviction in the Chinmaya Vision Programme (CVP) is validated through every academic and co-curricular pursuit.

The conduct of the students stands testimony to their firm faith in Indian culture, reinstilled in them. Value-based education and Geeta chanting have empowered all spiritually, making them judiciously ethical.

The vision and mission of the school remain indubitably in alignment with the four components of CVP. Along with academic excellence, the faculty also aims at identifying and chiselling the intrinsic potential of students.

Best wishes for future endeavours.

Mr. Sanjay Sinha

Chairman, Vidyalaya Managing Committee

Secretary

Mr. Vishnu Chandra Dixit

Hon'y Secretary, Vidyalaya Managing Committee

Hari Om

Humble salutations to Param Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayanandaji, Pujya Guruji Swami Tejomayanandaji and Swami Swaroopanandaji — Head, Chinmaya Mission Worldwide.

Vidya Bharati Chinmaya Vidyalaya has traversed 40 years of prominence in society. Every year of its existence remains adorned with accolades in multifarious arenas.

Whether in academic excellence or in the arena of co-curricular activities, the diligence of VBCV faculty and students remains noteworthy. The tenets of CVP remain loyally followed and practised in every event inside and outside the classroom.

Every initiative taken converges to the ultimate aim of developing judicious and morally upright global citizens. The seventeenth edition of The Epoch is another step towards that endeavour.

It manifests the plethora of opportunities provided to students to validate their acumen and creativity. It will remain a motivation for students to exhibit their potential without inhibition in the coming days.

Mr. Vishnu Chandra Dixit

Secretary, Vidyalaya Managing Committee

Vice-Chairman

Mr. B. Surendranath

Hon'y Vice-Chairman, Vidyalaya Managing Committee

Hari Om

When Sage Vashishta told Bharat to take over as the King of Ayodhya, Bharat told him that he wanted to go to the forest to bring Sri Rama back.

Sage Vashishta said that Bharat would rewrite the code of Dharma for all to follow in future. Students of Vidya Bharati Chinmaya Vidyalaya, Telco, will set new standards of education for future generations.

Mr. B. Surendranath

Vice-Chairman, Vidyalaya Managing Committee
