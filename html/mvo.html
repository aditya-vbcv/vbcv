Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Our Vision

To provide excellence in education, and to mould students who are professionally sound, morally upright and responsible citizens — an asset to the nation.

Our Mission

The school will work towards imparting value-based education through all lessons — curricular and co-curricular — to shape individuals into responsible global citizens.

Our Objectives

  1. To offer children a value-based and holistic education which paves the way for the integrated development of the physical, mental, intellectual and spiritual aspects of the personality, enriched by knowledge of Indian culture, a feeling of patriotism and a universal outlook.
  2. To mould children into young men and women of moral strength who can face the challenges of modern life with a smile, and make a difference in the world through their positive contribution.
  3. To give a practical and judicious combination of academic excellence, extra-curricular pursuits, character building and personality development.

School Motto

Keep Smiling

This is a lesson which we want all members of the Vidyalaya Parivar to learn and live through life. This little lesson, in its brevity, holds the essence of success in life. If we keep smiling through all the ups and downs of life, remaining untouched by joy or sorrow, can we successfully continue our journey towards knowledge?
